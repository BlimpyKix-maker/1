# Title options

The game now covers film, television, streaming, music, podcasts, online video, stage and comedy, and the business
around them. These titles try to cover all of it rather than film alone. The owner's current favourites are
**Apple Box** and **Slate**. Every name here needs a trademark and app-store search before it is chosen: short
common words are often taken in games or media.

## Backstage words every medium shares
- **Green Room**: the waiting room in a theatre, a TV studio, a comedy club or a concert venue.
- **Call Time**: when you have to be there. Film sets, TV studios, theatres and tours all use it.
- **Places**: the stage manager's call before curtain ("Places, please").
- **Half Hour**: the theatre call thirty minutes before the show. It also hints at a TV slot.
- **Stage Door**: where the fans wait and the performers come and go.
- **The Wings**: just offstage, waiting for your moment.
- **Soundcheck**: musicians, podcasters, TV and live comedy all do one.
- **Load-In**: the first job of every gig, show and shoot.
- **Standby**: the word before "go".
- **Cue**: the signal to start, in every medium.
- **House Lights**: the moment the audience can see itself.
- **Dressing Room**
- **Run of Show**: the running order for a broadcast, live event or concert.

## A shortlist
1. **Green Room**: every performer in every medium has sat in one.
2. **Call Time**: active, everyday on set and on stage, and it hints at a career's timing.
3. **Big Break**: says what the game is about.
4. **Overnight Success**: a wry title for a game about twenty-year careers.
5. **Headliner**: works for music, comedy, stage and screen.
6. **Clean Slate**: keeps Slate and adds a meaning.
7. **Show People**: plain and warm, about the people.
8. **Places**: short, theatrical, unusual.
